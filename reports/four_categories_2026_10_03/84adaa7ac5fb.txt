Introduction\nL134@P4: The Zayandeh Rud basin is located in the central part of Iran, with\nL135@P4: geographical coordinates between 50º 24' to 53º 24' north longitudes and 31º 11' to\nL136@P4: 33º 42' north latitude (Fig.1). The area of the basin is about 42,000 km2. Esfahan\nL137@P4: province constitutes 87.7% of the Zayandeh Rud basin. In addition, rest of the basin is\nL138@P4: placed in Bakhtiyary and Yazd provinces. Esfahan is the capital of Esfahan province,\nL139@P4: which is one of the oldest world cities (Fig. 2).\nL140@P4: The river has provided the basis for centuries of important economic activity,\nL141@P4: including the growth and establishment of Esfahan itself as the former capital city of\nL142@P4: Persia. The region has been able to support a long tradition of irrigated agriculture, in\nL143@P4: order to meet the domestic needs for the basin substantial population and industrial\nL144@P4: demands.\nL145@P4: However, the agriculture sector being the main water consumer using more\nL146@P4: than 80% of the available water resources is heavily under pressure. Numerous factors\nL147@P4: including continued growth of urban population, development of new agriculture\nL148@P4: lands and rapid increases in industrial demands have caused water shortage since an\nL149@P4: half century ago. To overcome this problem, number of trans-basin water projects\nL150@P4: have been executed and exploited during last decades, but still there is normally\nL151@P4: insufficient water to irrigate the total irritable area. This has also resulted in reduction\nL152@P4: of water quality of the Zayandeh Rud, especially downstream the city of Esfahan.\nL153@P4: Deterioration of water quality causes problem for the ecosystem of the rivers and Gaw\nL154@P4: Khuny swamp as the final outflow point of the river.\nL155@P4: In addition to the internal changes and activities that are presently going on in\nL156@P4: the basin or can be predicted in future, there is an important external change due to\nL157@P4: climate change. Climate may change significantly and may, therefore, impact the\nL158@P4: water resources of the basin. The strategies that can be taken to mitigate its possible\nL159@P4: negative impacts are scope of this research work, which is part of the Dutch funded\nL160@P4: project ADAPT (Aerts and Droogers, 2002). This project is aimed to investigate and\nL161@P4: compare adaptation strategies to cope with climate change impacts in several parts of\nL162@P4: the world with different climate and economic situations. The selected basins for the\nL163@P4: ADAPT international project are:\nL164@P4: - Mekong, South-East Asia\nL165@P4: - Rhine, Western-Europe\nL166@P4: - Sacramento, USA\nL167@P4: - Syr Darya, Central Asia\nL168@P4: - Volta, Ghana\nL169@P4: - Walawe, Sri Lanka\nL170@P4: - Zayandeh, Iran\n--------------------------------------------------------------------------------\nInternal Error ()\nciteturn68view1 [wordlim: 200] Source: open({\"ref_id\":\"https://www.iwrm-zayandehrud.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/inter-3-Agricultural-Transformation.pdf\",\"lineno\":null}); Total lines: 1\n--------------------------------------------------------------------------------\nCDM: Prior Consideration of the CDM (https://cdm.unfccc.int/Projects/PriorCDM/notifications/index_html?s=5440)\nciteturn68view2 [wordlim: 200] Crawled: last month; Content type: text/html; Source: open({\"ref_id\":\"https://cdm.unfccc.int/Projects/PriorCDM/notifications/index_html?s=5440\",\"lineno\":null}); Total lines: 53\n--------------------------------------------------------------------------------\nInternal Error ()\nciteturn68view3 [wordlim: 200] Source: open({\"ref_id\":\"https://geopersian.com/en/projects\",\"lineno\":null}); Total lines: 1\n"
